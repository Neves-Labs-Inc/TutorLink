**Mechanics that affect wording:**

- `lib/settings/settings.ts:15` builds Settings page field labels from setting keys the same way (`min_booking_lead_hours` becomes "Min booking lead hours").
- There are no shared label files for most concepts; strings live inline in pages and components. The only `*-presentation`/label maps are `lib/booking-presentation/bookingPresentation.ts`, `lib/bookings/bookings.ts:30`, `lib/guardians/reminders.ts:23-57`, `lib/guardians/language.ts`, `lib/availability/availability.ts:13-26`, `lib/users/users.ts:37-42`, `lib/status-badge/statusTones.ts`.

## Top problems (most confusing first)

1. **Time off has two names.** Office sees "Exceptions" / "Add exception" / "Availability exceptions"; the Tutor sees "Time Off" / "requests" for the same `TutorException` record, and Office deletes what the Tutor "withdraws". "Exception" is code jargon with no `CONTEXT.md` entry.
2. **Settings labels are raw database keys.** "Min booking lead hours", "Login rate limit ip max attempts" etc. are generated from keys (`lib/settings/settings.ts:15`). None are explained or in the glossary.
3. **Badges show raw enum values.** `StatusBadge` humanises API values, so Office reads "Parse error", "Stuck", "Human", "Bot", "No login", "Will skip". Chat flags also put routine Office handoffs ("Booking request", "Question") and bot failures ("Stuck", "Parse error") in the same badge, under the unexplained word "Flag" and the action "Mark handled".
4. **Booking vs Session.** One `Booking` is "booking" on Bookings, Tutor and Guardian pages, but "session" on the Dashboard, Child page and every Tutor page ("My Sessions", "Session detail"). `CONTEXT.md` has no Session entry.
5. **Takeover state has three words.** Code "human" shows as the "Human" filter/badge, the thread says "Held by …", and `CONTEXT.md` says Takeover. "Transfer to me" has no glossary entry.
6. **Staff / Users / Office / "an admin".** The same people are "Users" (Users page), "Staff" (bookings) and "Tutors". Chat and reminder copy uses "Staff" for the Office group, and Tutor-facing copy says "an admin" where Managers act too. Login says "clients".
7. **Reschedule never appears.** The dashboard act `CONTEXT.md` calls Reschedule is labelled "Edit booking" (an _Avoid_ term).
8. **Booking reminder appears as "Weekly reminders" / "Reminders" / "Weekly reminder".** Consent wording ("Record opt-in", "Consent history", "Source: System", "START") and WhatsApp internals (template ids, error codes, "Meta", "the run") show through on Reminders.
9. **"Schedule" for Availability.** The Tutor nav "My Schedule" names the weekly pattern, which `CONTEXT.md` says to avoid.
10. **Overloaded actions:** "Add guardian" / "Add home" (create vs link), "Add subject" (create catalogue Subject vs assign to Tutor), "Evaluation" (booking kind vs assessment section), "Pending" (booking vs time-off request), "Notes" (three readers), "Subject" (email subject vs school Subject), "Active/Inactive" (different effects per object).
